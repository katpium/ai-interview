# Company Information: NovaForge Technologies

## 1. Basic Company Details

Company Name: NovaForge Technologies
Industry: Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Software, Data Analytics, and Business Automation
Founded: 2018
Headquarters: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
Company Size: Approximately 420 employees
Company Type: Private technology company
Primary Market: Business-to-business software
Website: www.novaforgetech.example
Company Slogan: Practical AI for smarter workplaces

NovaForge Technologies is a fictional technology company that builds AI-powered software for businesses, education providers, hiring teams, and customer support organizations. The company focuses on creating practical AI tools that help employees complete work faster, understand information more clearly, and make better decisions.

NovaForge does not try to replace human workers completely. Instead, the company designs AI systems that support people by automating repetitive tasks, organizing information, and providing useful recommendations.

---

## 2. Company Mission

NovaForge Technologies' mission is to make intelligent automation accessible, reliable, and useful for every modern workplace.

The company believes AI should be understandable, safe, and easy to use. Their products are designed to help employees, managers, and teams work more efficiently while keeping human judgment at the center of important decisions.

---

## 3. Company Vision

NovaForge aims to become a leading provider of practical AI platforms for businesses around the world. The company wants to help organizations use AI responsibly without requiring every employee to become a machine learning expert.

Their long-term vision is to create AI assistants that can work alongside people in departments such as human resources, customer service, operations, analytics, education, and training.

---

## 4. Company Values

### Practical Innovation
NovaForge values technology that solves real problems. The company prefers useful, reliable solutions over overly complex systems that are difficult to maintain.

### Human-Centered AI
NovaForge believes AI should support people, not replace human thinking. Their systems are designed to assist workers while leaving final decisions to humans.

### Trust and Responsibility
Because NovaForge works with company data, candidate information, and customer records, privacy and security are very important. Employees are expected to handle data carefully and follow ethical standards.

### Clear Communication
NovaForge encourages employees to explain technical ideas in simple language. Teams often include both technical and non-technical members, so clear communication is considered essential.

### Continuous Learning
AI technology changes quickly. Employees are expected to stay curious, learn new tools, and adapt to new technical challenges.

### Collaboration
Most products at NovaForge require cooperation between software engineers, data analysts, AI researchers, designers, product managers, and business teams.

## 5. Main Products

### NovaAssist
NovaAssist is an internal AI assistant platform for businesses. It helps employees search company documents, ask policy questions, and receive answers based on approved internal information.

Common use cases:
- HR policy questions
- IT support questions
- Employee onboarding
- Internal knowledge search
- Company training support
- Meeting summary assistance

Example:
An employee could ask, "How do I request vacation time?" and NovaAssist would answer using the company’s HR policy documents.

### NovaHire
NovaHire is an AI-supported interview and hiring platform. It helps recruiters and hiring managers prepare interviews, generate role-specific questions, summarize candidate answers, and organize feedback.

NovaHire does not make final hiring decisions. It is designed to support human recruiters, not replace them.

Common features:
- AI-generated interview questions
- Candidate answer transcription
- Interview summaries
- Skill-based feedback
- Role-specific question banks
- Structured scoring rubrics
- Recruiter review dashboard

Example:
A recruiter can upload a job description and a candidate CV. NovaHire then generates interview questions based on the role and candidate background.

### NovaVoice
NovaVoice is a speech-to-text and text-to-speech platform. It supports applications that need voice interaction, transcription, audio feedback, and accessibility tools.

Common use cases:
- AI interview simulations
- Customer service voice bots
- Meeting transcription
- Voice-based training tools
- Accessibility support

Example:
In an AI interview, NovaVoice can read a question aloud, record the candidate’s spoken answer, and convert the answer into text for analysis.

### NovaFlow
NovaFlow is a workflow automation tool that connects business applications. It helps companies automate repetitive processes between tools such as email, calendars, CRMs, spreadsheets, and ticketing systems.

Common use cases:
- Automatically creating support tickets
- Sending follow-up emails
- Updating dashboards
- Routing customer requests
- Creating task reminders

### NovaInsight
NovaInsight is a data analytics and business intelligence product. It helps companies turn raw data into dashboards, reports, and business recommendations.

Common use cases:
- Sales analysis
- Customer behavior analysis
- Employee performance reporting
- Product usage dashboards
- Customer satisfaction tracking
- Executive reports

Example:
A company can connect sales data to NovaInsight and automatically generate monthly revenue summaries with charts and recommendations.

## 9. Example Role: Junior Data Analyst

Role Title: Junior Data Analyst
Department: Data and Analytics

Role Summary:
A Junior Data Analyst at NovaForge helps collect, clean, analyze, and visualize business data. This role supports internal teams by creating reports, dashboards, and insights that help the company make better decisions.

The role is suitable for someone early in their data career who understands spreadsheets, SQL, Python basics, and data visualization tools.

Key Responsibilities:
- Clean and organize datasets
- Write basic SQL queries
- Create dashboards and reports
- Analyze customer, product, and business data
- Identify trends and patterns
- Check data for errors and missing values
- Communicate findings to non-technical teammates
- Support business teams with data requests
- Document analysis steps clearly
- Work with engineers and product managers to understand data needs

Required Skills:
- Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets
- Basic SQL
- Basic Python
- Data cleaning
- Data visualization
- Basic statistics
- Clear written communication
- Attention to detail

Preferred Skills:
- Tableau or Power BI
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Dashboard design
- Business analysis
- Understanding of KPIs
- Basic knowledge of databases

Example Day-to-Day Tasks:
- Cleaning a CSV file from a customer survey
- Writing a SQL query to count active users
- Creating a dashboard showing product usage trends
- Checking why a sales report number does not match another report
- Summarizing customer feedback into categories
- Presenting a short analysis to a product manager

What Success Looks Like:
A successful Junior Data Analyst at NovaForge produces accurate reports, asks good questions, checks their work carefully, and explains findings clearly. They do not need to know advanced machine learning, but they should be comfortable learning new tools and improving their technical skills.

## 11. AI Interview System Context

For an AI interview simulation, NovaForge Technologies can be used as the fictional company interviewing candidates.

The AI interviewer should behave like a professional recruiter or hiring manager. It should ask clear questions, listen to candidate answers, and provide respectful feedback.

The AI interviewer should not:
- Ask illegal or discriminatory questions
- Make final hiring decisions without human review
- Ask overly personal questions
- Reveal private company information
- Give unfair feedback based on irrelevant details

The AI interviewer should:
- Ask role-relevant questions
- Adjust difficulty based on seniority level
- Use the candidate CV as context
- Use company information as context
- Ask follow-up questions when useful
- Keep the interview structured
- Give concise and constructive feedback

---

## 12. Recommended Interview Structure

Maximum Questions: 6

Question 1: Introduction
Purpose: Understand the candidate’s background and communication style.
Example: "Tell me about yourself and your interest in this role."

Question 2: Company and Role Motivation
Purpose: Check whether the candidate understands the company and role.
Example: "Why are you interested in working at NovaForge Technologies as a Data Analyst?"

Question 3: Experience or Project Question
Purpose: Let the candidate explain relevant experience.
Example: "Can you describe a data project you worked on and what you learned from it?"

Transition Message:
This should not count as a question.
Example: "Thank you. I will now ask a few technical questions related to data analysis."

Question 4: Technical Question 1
Purpose: Test basic role knowledge.
Junior example: "How would you handle missing values in a dataset?"
Senior example: "How would you validate that a key business metric is reliable?"

Question 5: Technical Question 2
Purpose: Test practical problem-solving.
Junior example: "Can you explain the difference between filtering data with WHERE and grouping data with GROUP BY in SQL?"
Senior example: "How would you design a dashboard for executives who need to track product performance?"

Question 6: Closing or Problem-Solving Question
Purpose: Understand work habits and professionalism.
Example: "What would you do if your analysis produced a result that seemed incorrect?"

## 15. Candidate Evaluation Criteria

Communication:
Does the candidate explain ideas clearly? Can they structure answers in a way that is easy to follow?

Role Knowledge:
Does the candidate understand basic concepts required for the role?

Problem Solving:
Can the candidate explain how they would approach a task or challenge?

Data Accuracy and Responsibility:
Does the candidate show care when handling data? Do they mention checking work, validating results, or avoiding unsupported conclusions?

Learning Ability:
Does the candidate show willingness to learn new tools and improve?

Company Fit:
Does the candidate understand NovaForge’s mission and values? Do they show interest in responsible AI and practical business tools?

Professionalism:
Does the candidate answer respectfully and stay focused on the question?

---

## 16. Scoring Rubric

Score each category from 1 to 5.

1 - Weak:
The answer is unclear, incomplete, or unrelated to the question.

2 - Developing:
The answer shows some understanding but lacks detail or examples.

3 - Good:
The answer is clear and relevant, with basic examples or reasoning.

4 - Strong:
The answer is well-structured and includes specific examples or thoughtful reasoning.

5 - Excellent:
The answer is clear, specific, role-relevant, and shows strong understanding.

Suggested categories:
- Communication
- Technical understanding
- Problem-solving approach
- Role fit
- Learning mindset
- Professionalism

---

## 17. Sample Candidate Feedback Style

Feedback should be short, respectful, and specific.

Example:
"Your answer was clear and showed that you understand the importance of data cleaning. To improve, you could give a specific example of a time you handled missing or inconsistent data."

Another example:
"You explained your project well and connected it to the Data Analyst role. A stronger answer would include more detail about the tools you used and the impact of your analysis."
